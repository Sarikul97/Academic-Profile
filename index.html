About

About Me

I am a Ph.D. Scholar in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. My research lies at the intersection of Reliability Theory, Applied Probability, Mathematical Statistics, Survival Analysis, and Nonparametric Statistical Inference. My current research focuses on developing mathematical and statistical methodologies for coherent systems, multi-component stress–strength reliability models, stochastic ordering, weighted distributions, and goodness-of-fit testing, with applications to reliability engineering and applied probability.

Research

Research Expertise

My research integrates mathematical modeling, probability theory, and statistical inference to develop methodologies for reliability engineering and applied statistics.

Reliability Theory

Coherent systems, stress–strength reliability, redundancy allocation, active and standby redundancy, system lifetime analysis.

Applied Probability

Stochastic ordering, copula models, random effects models, survival analysis, dependence modeling.

Statistical Inference

Nonparametric inference, U-statistics, goodness-of-fit testing, estimation, hypothesis testing.

Scientific Computing

R, Mathematica, Python, C Programming, LaTeX.

Current Research

Weighted distributions, multi-component stress–strength models, coherent systems, symmetry testing, reliability estimation.

Applications

Reliability engineering, risk analysis, survival analysis, engineering statistics, industrial systems.
